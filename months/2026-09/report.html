Executive Summary

September was a profit-recovery month driven by the cost lines, with a revenue dip and a cash-collections miss. Total revenue came in at $151,229 (−$11,664 / −7.2% MoM off August's restated $162,892; −$15,281 / −9.2% vs. September budget of $166,510). Subscriptions ($148,279) were −6.7% MoM and −7.9% vs. budget; professional services ($2,950) were −25.3% MoM and −46.4% vs. budget. Of the $10.7K subscription decline, $6.2K is customer cancellations and downgrades (Crystorama cancelled, −$2.5K; Buster & Punch cancelled part of its plan, −$0.9K; lower user counts across other accounts, −$2.8K) and $5.2K is one-offs from August that did not repeat, partly offset by +$0.7K of other movements.

COGS came in at $13,228 — down 24.3% MoM and 12.8% under budget — on lower support software (−$1.8K), hosting (−$1.5K) and SaaS tools (−$0.6K). Gross margin rose to 91.3% (+2.0pp MoM, +0.4pp vs. the 90.9% budget).

OpEx came in at $108,590 (−9.4% MoM, −3.9% vs. budget). August's Bruce payout did not repeat (Engineering Payroll −$20.4K, benefits −$2.7K). His replacement, Lucas, is booked in Engineering Consulting, which rose $8.3K including Olga's $1,050 September bill — we hedged for his fee in Contract Labor (61075), which stays flat at $12,500, but shifted it to Engineering Consulting. Marketing Consulting rose $3.7K to $9,830 (Vilni — UX and design-system work for product, approved by Kjael); the budget moves to $6,000/month from October. Sales Payroll fell $1.2K as commission eased ($4,869 in Aug → $2,757 in Sep).

Net effect: operating profit came in at $29,410 (+15.0% MoM, −23.4% vs. budget of $38,376) and net income at $6,969 (+92.0% MoM, −61.1% vs. budget of $17,938) — $12,668 below the $19,637 forecast in the August report.

Cash — bank cash rose $129,103 → $132,571 (+$3,468) against a forecast of +$10,550: a $7,082 shortfall that is a collections story, not a spending story. $10,405 of invoices were still open at September 30: Crystorama $2,540 (cancelled; unlikely to pay), Groupe Courchesne $3,190 (two invoices of $1,595), America's Backyards $2,175 (three invoices of $725) and Lib & Co $2,500 (implementation fee). A further $4,287 of September receipts were deposits in transit (Undeposited Funds) and land in October. Amex autopay ($38,717) went out 9/17 as forecast, and a $24,540 customer prepayment landed 9/18 as modeled. Cash/MRR improved 0.81× → 0.89×.

Forward look (single-track forecast, no new logos — worst case): the Master Forecast was rebuilt from actual September. October revenue is built account-by-account from the pricing-migration cohort and held flat; large renewal payments land on their contract dates. The model projects cash of $140,308 at end of October and $198,117 by December, with net income of roughly $33–35K a month.

Headline: Costs came in well under budget and under last month; the miss against the cash forecast is uncollected invoices ($10.4K), not spend.
